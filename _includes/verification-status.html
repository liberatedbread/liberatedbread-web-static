{%- comment -%} "Not yet verified on hardware" status block. Rendered by _layouts/device.html for every device guide that has NOT made an explicit, evidenced claim to have been run on the physical device. See the gate in the layout: a guide must set `hardware_verified: true` AND carry a `last_verified` date to suppress this. Omission is never enough. WHY IT FAILS SAFE ----------------- Unverified is the ORDINARY state of a guide here — submitting one you have not run on hardware is allowed and expected, and verification is marked later by whoever does have the device. That makes the default the common case, not the exception, which is exactly why the flag has to point the way it does: with most of the catalogue unverified, a scheme where silence meant "verified" would mislabel almost everything. The affirmative claim is the thing you write down; a guide cannot become verified by omission. TONE — this is a STATUS, NOT A DEFECT NOTICE. It describes the normal condition of most pages on the site, so it must not read as an apology, a health warning, or a reason to stop reading. State what is and is not confirmed, say what to do about it, and offer the reader the obvious way to help. Nothing here should suggest the guide is low quality: an unverified guide is researched and reviewed, just not yet run. Colour note: deliberately NOT the red of _includes/safety-block.html. That block owns the site's one escape from the bread palette, on the reasoning in its own comment that "a warning that reads as just more amber is not a warning"; a second red card directly under it would spend exactly the signal that reasoning protects. It is not amber either — amber is the site's attention/heading colour, and on a block that appears on nearly every guide that reads as permanent caution. Sky is the informational tone (7.22:1 on base), already used for type badges and code, and says "here is a fact about this page" rather than "be careful". The geometry matches the safety block so it still reads as native furniture. {%- endcomment -%}
